## The AFC Landscape & Top Tier Expectations
While most people and sportsbooks are looking at teams like Buffalo, Baltimore, or Kansas City to come out of the AFC, looking at all 17 weeks, a crazy scenario emerges where an 11-6 team completely misses the playoffs because of a 3-way tie for the last 2 wild card spots. There is also a dark horse priced around +1300 with the firepower to make a run to and potentially win the first Super Bowl in franchise history.
Before we get into the playoff picture, let’s take a step back and look at the popular AFC favorites, and why they won't make it happen.

## Why the Favorites Fall Short
## Buffalo Bills –
On paper, this team has what it takes to make a deep playoff run. Josh Allen will keep his team in the postseason conversation all year, and adding DJ Moore gives him an elite No. 1 option. The issue isn’t their roster, it’s a schedule with just enough landmines to spark a disaster.  That includes dropping six key matchups home against Detroit, the Chargers, and Kansas City, plus road trips to the Rams, Patriots, and Broncos.
Those six losses put them at 11-6 and second in the AFC East behind a 14-3 New England team.  This would lead to Buffalo needing a wild card spot to get into the playoffs, which is where they run into an issue, with the Chargers and Broncos both finishing 13-4 to lock up the top wild-card spot, the Bills end up in a three-way tie at 11-6 alongside the Chiefs and Bengals. Unfortunately for Bills Mafia, the tiebreaker math doesn't lean their way which leaves an 11-6 Buffalo squad on the outside looking in.
## Kansas City Chiefs – 
The Kansas City Chiefs dynasty window may finally be showing cracks. Patrick Mahomes keeps them competitive in any matchup, but with the AFC West looking stronger than ever, Kansas City faces a far tougher road than in years past.  The Chiefs start the year hot with four straight wins vs Denver, Indianapolis, Miami, and Las Vegas. This puts them at 4-0 going into the bye week.
 After the bye week, they finish the year 7-6 with a final record of 11-6 and third in the AFC West behind the Chargers and Broncos.  Those six losses coming against the LA Chargers, Seattle, Denver, LA Rams, New England, and San Francisco.  That 11-6 record puts them in the playoffs as the 7 seed just barely getting in over Buffalo.  Unfortunately for Kansas City, this would match them up with the eventual AFC champion in round 1, ending the Chiefs’ season much earlier than usual.
## Baltimore Ravens – 
Baltimore has a favorable schedule this year.  They drop only four games this year, which are home games against Cincinnati and the Chargers, and away games against Buffalo and Pittsburgh.  This 13-4 record locks up the AFC North for them, being two games ahead of Cincinnati, and sets them up as the third seed in the AFC playoff picture. 
Baltimore takes care of business against Cincinnati in Round 1 before running into the AFC winner in Round 2 where they could cause some trouble for them.  This matchup would probably be the AFC championship matchup if New England didn’t steal the top seed, but unfortunately for Baltimore that isn’t the case.  Therefore, they fall in round 2 and once again come up just short of a chance to make the Super Bowl for the first time since the 2012 season.

## The AFC powerhouse with +1300 odds to win the conference - 
Now that we’ve talked about the favorites to win the AFC and why they won't do that, let’s switch gears and get to the AFC Champion.  This team started last year 5-4 before winning out and finishing 13-4 to win their division.  They have a quarterback who is finally going to make his final jump to MVP level.  Last year in that 8-0 run to finish the year he averaged 251.13 passing yards per game and 2.4 passing touchdowns per game.  

This team is the Jacksonville Jaguars, although they lost some key pieces like Travis Etienne and Devin Lloyd, Bhayshul Tuten is more than capable of taking over Etienne’s role with very little if any drop off.  Replacing Devin Lloyd is a tough task and Ventrell Miller will need to make a few steps forward to prove he deserves the starting job, but he should be able to adjust just fine and make a bigger impact than he has in previous years.  In the receiving room, Parker Washington looks ready to become Trevor Lawrence’s primary target alongside Brian Thomas Jr., Jakobi Meyers, and emerging tight end Brenton Strange. Defenses will face a nightmare trying to cover all four options.  With this firepower, Trevor Lawrence has a golden opportunity to win MVP, projecting for 4,675 passing yards with 45 passing touchdowns and 7 rushing touchdowns.

The Jaguars finish 14-3, with home losses to New England and road drops at Baltimore and Houston.  This would have them tied for first in the AFC with New England who would take the tiebreaker leaving Jacksonville as the 2 seed and a first round matchup vs Kansas City.  As good as Patrick Mahomes is, the Chiefs aren’t as good as they used to be.  The Jaguars take care of business with 273 passing yards and 4 total touchdowns from Trevor Lawrence, moving them on to Round 2.  

Their Round 2 matchup would most likely be against Baltimore.  This would be a tougher matchup for them, but Baltimore’s defense can't stop this Jaguars offense, and Jacksonville wins this game 27-20.  Finally, this brings us to the AFC Championship where the Jaguars will have to face off with the defending AFC Champions in New England.  This is an opportunity for Drake Maye to make it back to the Super Bowl and prove the easy schedule allegations even more wrong than he already has in earning the 1 seed with a much tougher schedule.  New England’s defense again can't stop this Jacksonville offense, and the Jaguars pull this one out as well winning 38-35.  Taking them to the Super Bowl for the first time in franchise history with a chance to take down one of the NFC’s Juggernauts.  We will get back to the Super Bowl matchup after we talk about the NFC.
