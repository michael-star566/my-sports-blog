**<a id="ne-sea"></a>New England Patriots at Seattle Seahawks – 9/9 8:20 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;I've been going back and forth on this matchup. When the schedule first dropped, I leaned toward the Seahawks. The more I thought about it, I started to think the Patriots could pull this off. The Patriots added AJ Brown this offseason which makes Drake Maye’s passing game even more dangerous than last year. Seattle does have the better defense, and I do see this game being a shootout, but I trust Maye and the Patriots' offense to execute down the stretch. **New England wins, 34–31.**

**<a id="sf-lar"></a>San Francisco 49ers at LA Rams – Melbourne Game, 9/10 8:35 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;I like the Rams in this international matchup in Melbourne. LA's offseason moves make them tough to bet against here, especially with the shocking news that Aaron Donald came out of retirement. Pairing Donald with the newly added Myles Garrett gives the Rams a terrifying defensive line. On the other side, reigning MVP Matthew Stafford continues right where he left off last season, keeping this offense at a high level. San Francisco will put up a fight, but I trust Stafford and the Rams to secure the victory. **Los Angeles wins, 31–21.**

**<a id="cle-jax"></a>Cleveland Browns at Jacksonville Jaguars – 9/13 1:00 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;The Jaguars should cruise in this one. Trevor Lawrence starts his year off strong, throwing 2 touchdowns and 274 yards while adding another score on the ground. On the other side, Deshaun Watson and the Browns struggle to get anything going. Watson finishes the game with 164 passing yards, 1 touchdown, an interception, and a lost fumble. The Jaguars defend their home turf. **Jacksonville wins, 27–17.**

**<a id="tb-cin"></a>Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Cincinnati Bengals - 9/13 1:00 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;Give me Tampa Bay in a close one here. The Bengals are by far the better team, but they are also notoriously slow starters in the early weeks of the season. Tampa Bay's quarterback Baker Mayfield, from his days back in Cleveland, knows a thing or two about walking into Cincinnati and pulling off an early season upset. The Buccaneers take advantage of the sluggish start. **Tampa Bay wins, 24–17.**

**<a id="no-det"></a>New Orleans Saints at Detroit Lions - 9/13 1:00 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;Expect the Lions to take this matchup, though Tyler Shough and the Saints offense show off some great potential. Detroit's playmakers step up when it counts, with Jahmyr Gibbs having a strong game and finding the end zone once, while Amon-Ra St. Brown gets in on the scoring as well. The Lions pull away in the end. **Detroit wins, 31–24.**

**<a id="nyj-ten"></a>New York Jets at Tennessee Titans - 9/13 1:00 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;This shapes up to be a close game but give me the Titans. Tennessee's new-look offense clicks, with Cam Ward finding a rhythm and building instant chemistry with his new receivers, rookie Carnell Tate and free agent addition Wan'Dale Robinson from the Giants. On the other side, Geno Smith doesn't look too bad under center for the Jets, doing enough to give the team a little bit of hope for the season. The Titans pull it out in a tight one. **Tennessee wins, 21–17.**

**<a id="bal-ind"></a>Baltimore Ravens at Indianapolis Colts - 9/13 1:00 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;Look for the Ravens to come out on top here. Lamar Jackson continues to show why he's one of the best quarterbacks in the league, finding Zay Flowers for a touchdown while also rushing for a score of his own, and Derrick Henry finds the end zone as well. For Indianapolis, despite coming off an Achilles tear, Daniel Jones keeps them competitive, showing off a promising offense. Ultimately, Baltimore's firepower is just too much. **Baltimore wins, 35–27.**

**<a id="atl-pit"></a>Atlanta Falcons at Pittsburgh Steelers - 9/13 1:00 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;This matchup feels like a bit of a snooze fest, but the Steelers should walk away with it. Atlanta struggles to find any rhythm on offense as Tua Tagovailoa has a rough outing, though Kyle Pitts and Bijan Robinson still manage to account for the Falcons' two touchdowns. On the other side, Pittsburgh's offense is led by Aaron Rodgers, who connects with Michael Pittman Jr. for a passing touchdown while Rico Dowdle punches in a rushing touchdown. The Steelers add a field goal late in the fourth quarter that gives them the lead, leaving Atlanta no time to recover. **Pittsburgh wins, 17–14.**

**<a id="chi-car"></a>Chicago Bears at Carolina Panthers - 9/13 1:00 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;The Bears are my pick for this game. Although I like Carolina this year, they start the season off slow and Chicago takes advantage. Caleb Williams picks right up from where he left off at the end of last season, throwing for 236 yards and 3 touchdowns, while running back Kyle Monangai punches in the final score for the Bears on the ground. Meanwhile, the Panthers struggle on offense, with their only touchdown coming on a pass from Bryce Young to Jalen Coker. **Chicago wins, 28–13.**

**<a id="buf-hou"></a>Buffalo Bills at Houston Texans - 9/13 1:00 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;Go ahead and lock in the Bills here. Josh Allen, new star receiver DJ Moore, and running back James Cook put together a dominant performance against a stout Houston defense. While that Texans unit causes some trouble for Buffalo's offense throughout the day, Houston's offense simply doesn't have enough firepower to keep pace. **Buffalo wins, 21–17.**

**<a id="mia-lv"></a>Miami Dolphins at Las Vegas Raiders - 9/13 4:25 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;The Raiders should edge this one out in a tight contest. Kirk Cousins has a bumpy regular season debut for Las Vegas, while Malik Willis puts together a promising debut for the Dolphins but ultimately comes up just short. Miami's De'Von Achane has a huge game on the ground with two rushing touchdowns, but the Raiders' offense does just enough down the stretch. **Las Vegas wins, 24–21.**

**<a id="gb-min"></a>Green Bay Packers at Minnesota Vikings - 9/13 4:25 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;Expect a close game, but give me the Packers in this low scoring divisional game. Minnesota's defense looks just as dominant as it did last year, but Kyler Murray struggles to get things going for the Vikings' offense in his regular season debut with the team. On the other side, Jordan Love and the Green Bay offense grind out tough yards and do just enough to scrape by down the stretch. **Green Bay wins, 17–10.**

**<a id="was-phi"></a>Washington Commanders at Philadelphia Eagles - 9/13 4:25 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;The Eagles are the clear pick for this matchup. DeVonta Smith seizes the spotlight to show just how good he is now that A.J. Brown is gone, and Saquon Barkley puts together a great show on the ground. The Commanders do their best to stay competitive with Terry McLaurin and Stefon Diggs flashing plenty of potential as a new duo, and Jayden Daniels showing off his usual upside, but Philadelphia's offense ultimately overpowers Washington's defense. **Philadelphia wins, 27–17.**

**<a id="ari-lac"></a>Arizona Cardinals at Los Angeles Chargers - 9/13 4:25 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;Roll with the Chargers in this one. New offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel shows off an electric scheme in LA, and Justin Herbert looks primed for a great regular season by finding Ladd McConkey for a touchdown while the running back room punches in two more on the ground. Meanwhile, the Cardinals look dysfunctional overall, outside of a touchdown pass to Michael Wilson and a great game from Trey McBride. Arizona manages to get some points on the board but ultimately falls short. **Los Angeles wins, 31–14.**

**<a id="dal-nyg"></a>Dallas Cowboys at New York Giants – 9/13 8:20 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;Watch out for an upset here, as the Giants take this high scoring game. While the Cowboys' offensive firepower remains dangerous with Dak Prescott putting up his usual big numbers, New York makes a major jump this year under new head coach John Harbaugh. With a scary young roster featuring Jaxson Dart, Cam Skattebo, Malik Nabers, and Isaiah Likely, the Giants' offense gives Dallas all they can handle. Ultimately, the Giants' defense does just enough to get crucial stops when needed. **New York wins, 34–27.**

**<a id="den-kc"></a>Denver Broncos at Kansas City Chiefs – 9/14 8:15 PM**

&emsp;&emsp;It is hard to bet against the home team here, so give me the Chiefs. Patrick Mahomes returns from the ACL tear he suffered last year, and the Kansas City offense finds ways to exploit a phenomenal Denver defense that starts the game slow. On the other side, Bo Nix looks solid, finding Courtland Sutton and new addition Jaylen Waddle for touchdowns, though it won't go down as his best game of the year. That's all the Broncos' offense can manage, and the Chiefs do just enough to secure the victory. **Kansas City wins, 24–14.**
