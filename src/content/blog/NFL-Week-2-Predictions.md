<h2 style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.75rem;">Week 2 Predictions and Storylines</h2>

<h3 style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.35rem;">Intro</h3>

<p style="text-indent: 2rem;">Week 1 always lies. Early overreactions, fluky box scores, and title hopefuls look either completely invincible or suddenly broken. But Week 2 is where the dust settles, the data recalibrates, and real identities begin to surface.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 2rem;">This slate wastes no time putting those opening-weekend assumptions to the test. It all ignites under the Thursday night lights with a heavyweight collision between Detroit’s relentless ground attack and Josh Allen’s explosive Bills playing their first game in their new stadium in Orchard Park. From there, the storylines only intensify, a fierce NFC North rivalry where Chicago’s surging offense collides with Minnesota’s elite defense, a premier AFC quarterback match between Joe Burrow and C.J. Stroud in Houston, and a grueling situational test as Trevor Lawrence and the Jaguars head into the thin air of Denver.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 2rem;">Are Week 1 trends the new reality, or are we looking at classic early-season mirages? We’ve stripped away the public hype, processed the numbers through our projection models, and hunted down the true edges on the board. Here is the data-backed breakdown for Week 2.</p>

<h3 style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.35rem;">Detroit Lions at Buffalo Bills</h3>

<p style="text-indent: 2rem;">Detroit’s offense is built to keep pace in any environment. Jahmyr Gibbs is primed to replicate his opening-week dominance, projected to eclipse 100 rushing yards with at least one touchdown, and potentially a second. Through the air, Amon-Ra St. Brown should feast on high-percentage targets, while the game script sets up Jameson Williams for a long-ball touchdown.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 2rem;">But Detroit’s problem isn't scoring, it's stopping the bleeding defensively. The gaps they put on film last week are precisely what a phenomenal quarterback like Josh Allen is built to exploit. Allen looked unstoppable in his opener, and his connection with DJ Moore is already electric. Our data points to a massive night for Moore, projecting 6 receptions for 107 yards and two touchdowns. With James Cook modeled for a rushing score and Dalton Kincaid adding 4 receptions for 73 yards, Buffalo will dictate a relentless pace.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 2rem;">When the dust settles in Orchard Park, expect an absolute track meet. Detroit’s defensive liabilities are simply too glaring to survive four quarters of a shootout against this caliber of quarterback play.</p>

<p><strong>Prediction:</strong> Bills 38–31</p>
