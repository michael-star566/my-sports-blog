<h2 style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.75rem;">Thursday Night Football Week 3</h2>

<h3 style="text-align: center; font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.35rem;">Atlanta Falcons at Green Bay Packers</h3>

<p><strong>Prediction:</strong> Falcons 27–24<br>
<strong>Best Bet:</strong> Drake London 70+ Receiving Yards</p>

<p style="text-indent: 2rem;">Thursday Night Football heads to Lambeau Field this week, and the defining storyline is the long-awaited quarterback change in Atlanta. After watching Cooper Rush and Jack Strand combine for a disastrous five interceptions over the first two games, the Falcons are finally handing the keys to Michael Penix Jr. This switch instantly raises the ceiling of a passing game that hasn't found any rhythm this season. The biggest beneficiary is Drake London. Penix trusts his arm and isn't afraid to attack single coverage down the field, which sets London up for a massive target share. Our model loves London to clear his 70-yard prop with ease, projecting him for 7 catches, 89 yards, and a score as Penix settles in with a clean 207-yard, two-touchdown debut. To keep the Green Bay pass rush honest, Atlanta will also rely heavily on Bijan Robinson. Expect a heavy dual-threat workload from the backfield, with Bijan projected to rack up 87 rushing yards and 47 receiving yards on 5 catches, finding the end zone once on the ground and once through the air.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 2rem;">Green Bay has the offensive firepower to answer back. Because the Packers' backfield has struggled to generate reliable early-down efficiency, the scoring burden falls directly on Jordan Love. He is well-equipped to handle it against this Atlanta secondary. Expect Love to orchestrate long drives from the pocket, finishing with 246 passing yards and three touchdowns. Christian Watson remains his primary weapon when striking deep or attacking the red zone, projecting out to 4 catches for 87 yards and a pair of scores. When the Packers need to stay on schedule, Matthew Golden (6 grabs, 63 yards, 1 TD) and tight end Tucker Kraft (3 catches, 36 yards) will handle the underneath traffic. Green Bay will score in bunches, but Penix unlocking Atlanta's playmakers gives the Falcons just enough punch to pull off a 27–24 road upset.</p>
